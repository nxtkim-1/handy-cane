# HandyCane v2 — Parametric CAD Suite, Print Files & Verification

This directory contains the reproducible **OpenCascade B-Rep CAD suite** (`build123d`), automated clearance/physics verification script, print-ready `.stl` / `.3mf` files, multi-view studio renders, and formal A3 General Arrangement drawing for **HandyCane v2**.

> **Evidence Hygiene**: Geometric clearances, volumes, Center-of-Mass (CoM), and rotational inertia values are **`[CAD/ANALYTICAL]`** (verified directly on the exported B-Rep STEP and watertight mesh files). Physical ISO 11334-4 static/cyclic rig testing, IP54 water-spray testing, acoustic SPL measurements, and human fall-trial validation remain **`[PHYSICAL-PENDING]`**.

---

## Directory Layout

- [`build_handycane_v2.py`](build_handycane_v2.py) — Master parametric B-Rep CAD generator (`CaneV2Params` dataclass).
- [`verify_cad_v2.py`](verify_cad_v2.py) — Independent read-only verification suite checking STEP validity, 2-manifold STL/3MF watertightness, `0.000 mm³` boolean overlap across all 120 component pairs, `BRepExtrema` minimum clearances, mass/CoM/rotational inertia vs v1, and structural load-path statics.
- [`render_handycane_v2.py`](render_handycane_v2.py) — Generates studio 3D renders, exact B-Rep cross-sections, ergonomic biomechanics diagram, physics charts, and the A3 General Arrangement sheet.
- [`verification_v2.json`](verification_v2.json) — Machine-readable verification report generated by `verify_cad_v2.py`.
- [`manifest_v2.json`](manifest_v2.json) — Complete part catalog, bounding boxes, volumes, print orientations, and explode vectors.
- `step/` — True solid B-Rep `.step` files:
  - `handycane_v2_assembly.step` — Full named multi-body assembly (native import for Autodesk Fusion 360, Onshape, SolidWorks).
  - Printable parts: `grip_upper.step`, `grip_lower.step`, `sos_button_cap.step`, `pod_front_shell.step`, `pod_rear_shell.step`, `epdm_shaft_liner.step`, `usbc_dust_plug.step`, `fit_test_coupon.step`.
  - Reference envelopes: `ref_cane_shaft.step`, `ref_ferrule_tip.step`, `ref_pcb.step`, `ref_imu_module.step`, `ref_battery_cell.step`, `ref_speaker_driver.step`, `ref_tactile_button.step`, `ref_usbc_port.step`, `ref_fasteners.step`.
- `stl_print_ready/` & `3mf_print_ready/` — Support-free, flat-bed-oriented (`min_z = 0.000 mm`) FDM manufacturing files.
- `renders/` & `drawings/` — Engineering visual package (`01`–`06` PNGs + vector `handycane_v2_ga.svg`).

## Design for Additive Manufacturing (DFM) & Slicer Checklist

Every printable part is engineered to print **flat on `z = 0.000 mm` with zero support material**:

| File Prefix | Part Name | Recommended Material | Print Orientation (`z = 0` Bed Face) | Walls / Infill | Supports | Key DFM Features |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `01_grip_upper` | Contoured Upper Handle | Matte ASA or PETG (Charcoal) | Split plane down (`z = 886.15 mm`) | 4 walls (1.6 mm), 35% gyroid | **None** | `45°` internal button-skirt cone; vertical M3 heat-set pockets |
| `02_grip_lower` | Contoured Lower Handle | Matte ASA or PETG (Charcoal) | Split plane down (`180°` X-flip) | 4 walls (1.6 mm), 35% gyroid | **None** | `45°` conical M3 counterbore seats; >= 4.2 mm wall below Al tang |
| `03_sos_button_cap` | Concave Tactile SOS Cap | PETG (Safety Coral `#D94F30`) | Flat retention skirt base down | 4 walls, 100% concentric | **None** | Enable top-surface ironing (`0.12–0.16 mm` layers) for smooth thumb dish |
| `04_pod_front_shell` | Concentric Front Spine (`+X`) | Matte ASA or PETG (Charcoal) | Flat front face down (`-90°` Y) | 4 walls (1.6 mm), 35% gyroid | **None** | Upward-facing PCB standoffs, IMU frame, speaker seat & M3 insert bosses |
| `05_pod_rear_shell` | Concentric Rear Spine (`-X`) | Matte ASA or PETG (Charcoal) | Flat rear face down (`+90°` Y) | 4 walls (1.6 mm), 35% gyroid | **None** | Upward-facing 18650 cradle, tongue-and-groove channel & `45°` M3 counterbores |
| `06_epdm_shaft_liner` | Captive Shaft Friction Sleeve | Shore 85A TPU (or 1.2 mm EPDM sheet) | Collar end flat on bed | 3 walls, 100% infill | **None** | Longitudinal `3.2 mm` C-slit snaps over Ø22 mm shaft without removing ferrule |
| `07_usbc_dust_plug` | USB-C Ingress Seal | Shore 85A TPU (Safety Coral) | Outer flange flat on bed | 3 walls, 100% infill | **None** | Stepped dual-lip bung seals bottom USB-C recess (`IP54` target) |
| `08_fit_test_coupon` | 22-Min Tolerance Gauge | Same filament & printer as shells | Flat bottom (`z = 0`) | 4 walls, 30% gyroid | **None** | Print **first** to check Ø24.7 mm liner bore, Ø4.70 mm M3 insert boss & 12.8 mm switch fit |

### Dimensional Tolerance & Hardware Rules
- **Minimum Structural Shell Thickness**: `>= 2.60 mm` on all exterior walls (`>= 4.20 mm` under ergonomic grip core; `2.80 mm` front electronics wall; `2.65 mm` rear battery cradle wall).
- **Heat-Set Brass Inserts**: `8× M3` standard tapered heat-set inserts (`OD 4.6 mm`: `4× L=4.0 mm` in `grip_upper`, `4× L=5.7 mm` in `pod_front_shell`). Pocket diameter `Ø4.70 mm` with `45°` lead-in/relief cones; set with a temperature-controlled soldering iron tip at `215–230 °C` (PETG) or `245–260 °C` (ASA).
- **Nominal Clearances (`[CAD/ANALYTICAL]`)**: `0.25–0.45 mm` radial clearance on electronic pockets and button aperture; `0.30 mm` handle clamping draw gap; `0.40 mm` concentric pod clamping draw gap.
